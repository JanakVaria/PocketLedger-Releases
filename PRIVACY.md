# PocketLedger privacy summary

PocketLedger keeps manually entered financial records in local app storage. It requests no internet, contacts, or broad storage permissions. It has no analytics, ads, sign-in, backend, or cloud synchronization. Android automatic cloud/device-transfer backups are disabled.

JSON backups and CSV exports are created only on your request, through the Android system file picker. The destination you choose can be local storage or a third-party/cloud provider. Such files contain your records; protect and back them up yourself. Uninstalling or clearing app storage removes the local database.

The GitHub download page and your selected file-storage provider have their own privacy policies. This summary describes the PocketLedger Android app, version 1.0.0.
